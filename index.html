TradeNotes Account Deletion

TradeNotes allows users to permanently delete their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the TradeNotes app.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your request.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Profile Information
  • Trade Journal
  • Trade Notes
  • Analytics
  • Uploaded Screenshots
  • Settings

Retention Policy

Once your deletion request is processed, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted.
